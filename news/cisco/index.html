This is Cisco's free Skills for All program (Cisco Networking Academy). It's a free, self-paced, mobile-friendly online learning platform. The course to start with is Introduction to Cybersecurity, and Cisco itself recommends starting there. (csrwire) (cisco)
What you get from that course
It has 5 modules, 4 quizzes and a final exam, and takes about 15 hours. (netacad)
You don't need any prior tech knowledge. (europa)
If you pass, you receive a Certificate of Completion and a Cisco digital badge you can share on LinkedIn and other networks. (netacad)
How to join, step by step
Go to the official site, skillsforall.com, by typing it into your browser yourself. Don't rely on the "Apply Now" link from a social media poster, since you can't be sure where it leads.
Find Introduction to Cybersecurity and click "Enroll".
Enter your name, email and a password. Use an email you check often, because your certificate will be tied to it.
Open your inbox, confirm your account, then log in. On some course portals you're asked to use "Forgot Password" to set your password through a link sent to your email. (netacad)
Work through all 5 modules, do the quizzes, then take the final exam.
Once you pass, download your certificate and badge from your profile.
Screens can differ a little, so follow the on-screen instructions on the site.
What comes next
If you want to go further, there's a "Junior Cybersecurity Analyst" pathway. It starts with Introduction to Cybersecurity, then a Career Path exam, then Cisco Certified Support Technician (CCST) Cybersecurity. I haven't confirmed whether the official CCST certification exam is free, so check the cost on the site before you commit. (arkansas)
Good to know: this course has existed for years, so it isn't new as the poster implies. It's still genuine and a solid starting point for beginners.
