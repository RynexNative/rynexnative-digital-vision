Apple outage hits iCloud, App Store as company rules out not cyberattack on 17 November 2026

Apple had a real outage on Tuesday, November 17, 2026. Here is what the reports show. Times are Eastern Time (ET), which is 8 hours behind Tanzania time.
What happened
Problems began late morning ET. Apple's status page first flagged AppleCare at 11:15 a.m. ET, and Downdetector reports spiked around 11:40 a.m. By just before 1 p.m., roughly 2,000 users were reporting outages on Apple Support, Apple Pay, and iCloud. (Time)
Services affected
iCloud sign-in: Apple's status page listed issues with iCloud Account & Sign In as affecting "all users". Users reported unexpected iCloud sign-outs on iPhone and Mac, followed by verification failures or unknown errors when trying to sign back in. (TechCrunch) (9to5Mac)
App Store: Others say the App Store won't load or download apps. Apple's page showed the App Store outage starting around 11:45 a.m. ET. (9to5Mac)
Apple Pay, Apple Music and Apple Support: Users reported problems with these too, though Apple's list of resolved outages did not always include them.
AppleCare: The status page said "AppleCare purchase is temporarily unavailable." (Time)
Developers: Developers have separately reported trouble accessing App Store Connect. (9to5Mac)
Other systems: Apple's status page also listed iCloud Mail, iCloud Web Apps (iCloud.com), Schoolwork, and the Volume Purchase Program. One report says Apple retail stores and corporate offices were also affected. (TechCrunch) (9to5Mac)
Cause
Apple has said an internal update caused the outage, ruling out a cyber attack. Before that statement, some people had suspected a cyberattack, but Apple's explanation contradicts that. Be aware that some reports describe Apple's statement only as an update, so the details of what changed have not been published. (9to5Mac)
Recovery
Consumer services came back over the afternoon. One report says Apple has resolved outages that impacted several of the company's services on Tuesday, including Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple Support. Timing differs between sources. For example, one says by 2:02 p.m., outages affecting the App Store and Apple Music had been resolved, while Apple's AppleCare outage was marked resolved at 3:39 p.m. ET. Developer services were still having problems at the time of the 9to5Mac update. (Time) (detroitnews)
Why it hurt so many people
Your Apple ID is used for iCloud, Apple Pay, the App Store, and setting up a new phone. When sign-in fails, people can be locked out of several services at once.
What you should do
Check Apple's System Status page to see which services are currently listed as available.
If an app download or purchase failed during the outage, try again now.
If you were signed out of iCloud, try signing in again. One outlet reported that you should not need to reset your password, since the cause was on Apple's side. If sign-in keeps failing, wait a while and try again later.
Also, a separate iCloud outage affecting Mail, Contacts, Calendar, and Find My was reported on November 10. It is not confirmed to be related to Tuesday's problemsWho was affected
iPhone, iPad, and Mac users who were signed in to iCloud or the App Store. Reports said users were signed out of iCloud and had trouble signing back in.
Apple Pay users, who had problems making payments.
Music and app users, who had trouble with Apple Music and downloading apps.
App developers, who had trouble getting into App Store Connect, the tool used to publish and manage apps.
Schools and businesses using Apple School Manager, the Volume Purchase Program, or Apple Business Manager.
Anyone trying to activate a new iPhone or buy AppleCare.
Reports came from the US and from other countries, but I did not find a source that confirms how many users were affected in Tanzania specifically.
How it affects an ordinary user
If your Apple ID is your main sign-in for your phone, you may have been asked for your password again or unable to open your iCloud photos, backups, or contacts for a while. You may also have had failed app downloads or payments. Most of these problems should be fixed once Apple's services are back, so you may not need to change anything.
How it affects a developer
If you publish apps or websites that depend on Apple services, a problem with App Store Connect can delay uploads, updates, and access to your account. Developer services were still having problems at the time of the 9to5Mac update.
Here is how this outage would affect you, depending on how you use Apple's services.
As an iPhone or Mac user
If you were signed out of iCloud, you may have had to sign in again. Your iCloud photos, contacts, and backups may have been slow to sync during the outage. App downloads and Apple Pay payments may have failed. Most of these should now be working again, so you probably only need to retry anything that failed.
As an app developer
This is the part that matters most if you publish apps. During the outage, App Store Connect was unreliable, so you may have been unable to:
upload new app builds
submit an app or update for review
manage TestFlight testers
check the status of an app that was already submitted
If you had a release planned for that day, it may now be delayed. Check your app's status in App Store Connect before assuming anything was submitted.
If your apps use Apple services
If your app uses Sign in with Apple, Apple Pay, or in-app purchases, some of your users may have hit errors on November 17. Check your logs or error reports for that day. If you see a spike in failed sign-ins or payments, it likely matches this outage, and you do not need to fix your code for it.
What I'd suggest
Check the developer status page before you upload or submit a build during any Apple outage.
If you are building a system that depends on Apple services, add retry logic so temporary failures do not break things for your users.
Apple says the outage is over. Its services were back to normal by the evening of Tuesday, November 17, 2026, and it was about a day ago at the time of my search.

What is still working through

Individual sign-ins: People who were signed out of iCloud may need to sign in again. If sign-in still fails, try again later, then contact Apple Support if the problem continues.
Failed downloads and purchases: Retry them now.
